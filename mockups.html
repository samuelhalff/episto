How Swiss Mail works

From your words to a posted letter — in three simple steps.

💬
Step 1

Describe

Tell us what you need in plain language — or dictate it. No formatting, no templates, no fuss.

📄
Step 2

Review

We draft a professionally structured letter. Edit anything — the subject, tone, every paragraph — until it's perfect.

📮
Step 3

Send

Pay once, no subscription. We submit your letter to Swiss Post for physical delivery.

Common use cases

🏋️ Lease cancellation Terminate your rental agreement properly
🛡 Insurance cancellation Cancel policies within the notice period
📋 Contract termination End any service contract formally
⚠️ Formal complaint Lodge a structured, professional complaint
📨 Official correspondence Communicate with authorities or institutions
⚖️ Demand letter Request payment or action formally

Editable zones

Hover over any zone in the preview to edit it. Changes update the letter in real time.

1
Sender block — Name, street, postcode, city
2
Return address — Single-line, above recipient window
3
Recipient block — 4–6 lines for envelope window
4
Date / reference — Place + date, optional ref
5
Subject line — Bold, 1–2 lines
6
Salutation — Formal greeting
7
Body text — Main letter content
8
Closing — Formal sign-off
9
Signature — Typed name + optional image
10
Enclosure note — Optional attachments list
Template notes
  • A4 portrait (210 × 297mm)
  • Left margin: 25mm, right: 20mm
  • Fold marks at 99mm and 198mm
  • Recipient window at 55mm from top
  • Font: Source Serif 4, 10–11pt body
  • Max 5 pages per letter
